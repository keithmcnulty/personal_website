Bio

I'm a mathematician, an industrial-organisational psychologist and a data scientist. I hold a PhD in Pure Mathematics, and I've spent my career applying quantitative thinking to one of the hardest systems there is: human organisations.

I lead work on talent science and people analytics, bringing together I-O psychology and data science to help organisations use statistics, network science and machine learning to make better and fairer decisions about their people. I write regularly about data science, statistics and programming, and I've published two technical books on modelling in people analytics.

I speak internationally on the future of work, analytics and AI. When I'm not doing any of that, I'm usually back at the blackboard working on a problem in pure mathematics.

Mathematical research

My research is in algebraic combinatorics, currently on Kazhdan–Lusztig positivity of immanants: when certain matrix functions built from characters of the symmetric group are guaranteed to be non-negative, and why.
