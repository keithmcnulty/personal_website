Bio

I'm a mathematician by training and a people scientist by trade. I hold a PhD in Pure Mathematics, and I've spent my career applying quantitative thinking to one of the hardest systems there is: human organisations.

I lead work on talent science and people analytics, helping organisations use statistics, network science and machine learning to make better and fairer decisions about their people. I write regularly about data science, statistics and programming, and I've published two technical books on modelling in people analytics.

I speak internationally on the future of work, analytics and AI. When I'm not doing any of that, I'm usually back at the blackboard working on a problem in pure mathematics.
